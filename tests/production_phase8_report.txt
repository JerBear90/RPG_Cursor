9. Dread system
---------------
StatusEffectsComponent: dread buildup in deep darkness/haunted zones (DreadZone).
Threshold effects: reduced stamina/focus regen, screen-edge distortion hooks.
Dawnwatch shelter and ShadowShelterZone slow/stop buildup.

10. Shadow Exposure system
--------------------------
Buildup from ShadowPoolZone, umbral enemies, dark areas.
Threshold may apply Umbral Sickness (bounded health deg, reduced healing, focus cap).
ShadowShelterZone and protective light decay exposure.

11. HUD feedback
----------------
player_hud.gd: DreadExposureBar + ShadowExposureBar in existing needs row.
Hidden when irrelevant; distinct violet palette; threshold warning colors.
Does not overwrite heat/cold/blight/coastal indicators.

12. Protective light behavior
-----------------------------
ShadowShelterZone (Dawnwatch, explicit ward metadata): slows dread, decays shadow,
reduces visual darkness contribution via status pipeline.
Decorative lights are NOT treated as wards unless zoned.

13. Eclipse weather
-------------------
EclipseStormController: normal twilight, heavy shadow fog, eclipse surge, umbral storm,
clearing period. Surges increase dread, may strengthen enemies, expand shadow pools.
Dawnwatch shelter suppresses weather effects inside ward volume.

14. Shadow traversal
--------------------
ShadowGroundZone: movement resistance, reduced stamina recovery, shadow buildup,
visual distortion. Jump/dodge remain functional; no infinite slowdown.

15. Hazard implementation
---------------------------
Overworld: shadow pools, dread zones, shadow ground patches.
Dungeon: curse sigils (DungeonTrap), shadow traps in Dread Maze, nightglass hazards.
Telegraphed via existing damage/status pipelines with cooldowns.
Spawn markers placed outside hazard volumes.
